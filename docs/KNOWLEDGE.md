# Producer knowledge: sources and rationale

`src/library/knowledge.ts` is the compact pack the agent reads on every run.
This page records where each claim came from so the pack can be audited and
extended. Patches live in `src/library/patches.ts`, grids in
`src/library/patterns.ts`, harmony/arrangement in `src/library/theory.ts`.

## Harmony and songwriting
- Minor-key dominance, common keys, i–VI–III–VII / i–III–VII–VI / VI–VII–i–VI and 2-bars-per-chord: [Mixed In Key – Best chords for EDM](https://mixedinkey.com/captain-plugins/wiki/best-chords-for-edm/), [eMastered – 5 EDM progressions](https://emastered.com/blog/edm-chord-progressions), [Future Proof Music School – EDM progressions broken down](https://futureproofmusicschool.com/blog/unlocking-the-secrets-of-edm-chord-progressions), [LANDR – EDM/house/techno progressions](https://blog.landr.com/edm-chord-progression/).
- Andalusian cadence (major V from harmonic minor; ostinato over a tonic pedal; Phrygian descent variant): [Wikipedia – Andalusian cadence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andalusian_cadence), [Motifkit](https://motifkit.com/andalusian-cadence/), [Maxim's Guitar Workshop – modal interchange](https://maximsguitarworkshop.com/modal-interchange-andalusian-cadence-and-neapolitan-2/).
- Voicing: roots/5ths low, extensions up; rootless voicings over a sub; harmonic rhythm on beat 1/3: [Sample Focus – Chord voicing in EDM](https://blog.samplefocus.com/blog/chord-voicing-in-edm/), [Yamaha – pop/rock voicings](https://hub.yamaha.com/poprock-voicings-part-1).
- Tension/release, open cadences, melody rising into the chorus: [Secrets of Songwriting – Tension-Release principle](https://www.secretsofsongwriting.com/2010/02/15/the-tension-release-principle-of-songwriting/).
- Motif development (repetition, sequence, inversion, augmentation), hook rules: [MUC140 melody writing](https://paradisevalley.libguides.com/MUC140/melody-writing-dev), [Hyperbits – better melodies](https://hyperbits.com/blog/write-better-melodies/), [Your Ghost Production – producer melody guide](https://yourghostproduction.com/content/how-does-a-music-producer-write-a-melody).
- Stable/unstable scale degrees (chord-relative model): [Hooktheory](https://www.hooktheory.com/support/musicreference?concept=music-concepts-stable-unstable-scale-degree), [musicintro 3.3](https://musicintro.neocities.org/e-books/english/e-book1/3.3).
- Phrase symmetry (4/8 bars): [Joseph Riepel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Riepel).
- Counter-melody rules: [Secrets of Songwriting – countermelody](https://www.secretsofsongwriting.com/2011/11/07/5-steps-to-writing-an-effective-countermelody/), [Songcage](https://songcage.com/blog/countermelody/), species counterpoint via [ToneSavvy](https://tonesavvy.com/music-practice-exercise/241/first-species-counterpoint-game/).
- Arps (root-5-8-5 pluck ~50% gate, rolling 85%, one sustained note per bar): [Myloops – trance arpeggios](https://www.myloops.net/programming-trance-arpeggios-and-rhythmic-sequences), [Native Instruments – arpeggiators](https://blog.native-instruments.com/arpeggiators/).

## Bass
- Root/5th/octave shapes, 1-3-5-8, lock-or-answer the kick, "80% extension of chords/lead": [Yamaha – roots, fifths, octaves](https://hub.yamaha.com/guitars/bass/the-importance-of-roots-fifths-and-octaves-in-bass-playing/), [Hyperbits – writing bass lines](https://hyperbits.com/blog/writing-bass-lines/), [Mastering.com – how to write a bassline](https://mastering.com/how-to-write-a-bassline/), [Dogs On Acid – writing basslines](https://www.dogsonacid.com/threads/writing-basslines.797961/).
- 303 accents before the kick, slides every 3rd/4th note, velocity 60-70 vs 127: [Roland – mastering the TB-303 sequencer](https://articles.roland.com/mastering-the-tb-303-sequencer-in-roland-cloud/), [Monosounds – acid bass in Serum 2](https://monosounds.studio/acid-bass-serum-2/), [JonDent – famous 303 patterns](https://djjondent.blogspot.com/2020/04/famous-303-bassline-patterns-page-1.html).
- Reese: sustained notes, glide ~200 ms, movement from slow LFOs: [LANDR – Reese bass](https://blog.landr.com/reese-bass/), [Attack – Reese Bass Redux](https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/tutorials/reese-bass-redux/), [Dystopian Collective](https://www.thedystopiancollective.com/tutorials-2/how-to-create-reese-bass-the-complete-guide-to-the-iconic-drum-amp-bass-sound).
- Wobble LFO rates at 140 (1/4, 1/8, 1/16, dotted, triplet), doubling rate into the drop, one LFO → several targets: [MusicRadar – LFO wobble bass](https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/lfo-wobble-bass), [Monosounds – wobble bass](https://monosounds.studio/wobble-bass-serum-2/), [KVR – Skrillex wobble](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=301715).

## Drums and groove
- House/techno 909 grid (kick 1,5,9,13; clap 5,13; open hat 3,7,11,15; closed 16ths with accents), choke behaviour, sparse clap: [Aulart – Detroit techno on the TR-909](https://www.aulart.com/blog/create-a-detroit-techno-pattern-with-the-tr-909/), [Attack – Dave Clarke techno](https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/beat-dissected/how-to-program-techno-like-dave-clarke/), [Attack – Dark Berlin techno](https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/beat-dissected/dark-berlin-techno/), [Studio Brootle – techno patterns](https://www.studiobrootle.com/techno-drum-patterns-and-drum-programming-tips/).
- DnB 2-step (kick 1 and 11, snare 5/13, 16th hat pickups at low velocity): [Loopcloud – Tricks of the trade: DnB](https://www.loopcloud.com/cloud/blog/4978-Tricks-of-the-Trade-Drum-Bass), [BassGorilla – how to make DnB](https://bassgorilla.com/how-to-make-drum-and-bass/), [Mixgraph – DnB BPM distribution](https://www.mixgraph.io/bpm-for/drum-and-bass).
- Amen chopping / resequencing: [KAN Samples](https://kansamples.com/blogs/learn/how-to-chop-amen-break), [Woovebox amen tutorial](https://www.woovebox.com/support/guides--tutorials/sampler/amen-chop-tutorial).
- Dubstep halftime (snare on 3, 2-bar loop with variation): [Sample Focus – 140 BPM](https://samplefocus.com/blog/everything-you-need-to-know-about-140-bpm/), [KAN – dubstep arrangement](https://kansamples.com/blogs/learn/dubstep-track-arrangement), [Studio Brootle – dubstep patterns](https://www.studiobrootle.com/dubstep-drum-patterns/).
- Trap hats (8ths + 1/32 rolls before snares, crescendo, no swing), 808 slides, drill step-11 placement: [Audeobox – trap in Ableton](https://www.audeobox.com/learn/ableton/how-to-make-trap-beats-in-ableton/), [bap.studio – trap hi-hat pattern](https://bap.studio/patterns/trap-hi-hat-pattern/), [Padwolf](https://padwolf.app/learn/how-to-make-trap-beats/).
- UKG 2-step (kick skips 2/4, swing 55–65%, rims): [Orphiq – UK garage](https://orphiq.com/resources/what-is-uk-garage), [MusicRadar – UKG tutorial](https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/uk-garage-tutorial), [Attack – Garage Shuffle](https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/beat-dissected/garage-shuffle/).
- Footwork (tresillo kick runs, pitched 808 toms): [Orphiq – footwork](https://orphiq.com/resources/what-is-footwork-music), [MusicRadar – juke 808 kick](https://www.musicradar.com/tuition/tech/how-to-make-a-juke-style-pitched-808-kick-pattern-554083).
- Euclidean catalogue E(3,8) tresillo, E(5,8) cinquillo, E(7,12) bembé, E(5,16) bossa, E(3,7) ruchenitza, E(4,9) aksak; 7/8 groupings = E(3,7): [Toussaint – The Euclidean algorithm generates traditional musical rhythms](https://cgm.cs.mcgill.ca/~godfried/publications/banff.pdf), [Synthtopia – intro to Euclidean rhythms](https://www.synthtopia.com/content/2015/11/20/an-introduction-to-euclidean-rhythms/), [Product London – odd meter](https://www.productlondon.com/odd-meter/).
- IDM / drill 'n' bass (fast MIDI, tiny note lengths, ratchet rushes): [KVR – IDM glitch drums](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=541098), [Wikipedia – Drill 'n' bass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drill_%27n%27_bass). Burial's off-grid placement: [Gearspace – Burial techniques](https://gearspace.com/board/electronic-music-instruments-and-electronic-music-production/194386-burial-production-techniques.html).

## Arrangement
- Phrase grid, ABAB, section lengths by genre, drop at bar 32 for 140, DnB three-statement drops, techno without hard drops: [Cymatics – EDM song structure](https://cymatics.fm/blogs/production/edm-song-structure), [Hyperbits – the shape every record follows](https://hyperbits.com/blog/edm-song-structure/), [EDMtips](https://edmtips.com/edm-song-structure/), [Lord Reverb](https://lordreverb.com/production/song-structure-for-producers/), [Gearspace – arranging in electronic music](https://gearspace.com/board/electronic-music-instruments-and-electronic-music-production/1414602-arranging-electronic-music.html).

## Mix
- One element below ~40–100 Hz, kick 50–60 Hz, sidechain/multiband ducking, high-pass everything else at ~100–120 Hz, mono below 120 Hz, per-track −18…−12 dBFS, kick-first gain staging: [Beat Kitchen – mixing electronic music](https://beatkitchen.io/guides/electronic-music/09-mixing-electronic/), [Mastering The Mix – kick and bass](https://www.masteringthemix.com/blogs/learn/62370629-how-to-mix-kick-and-bass), [iZotope – balancing EDM kicks](https://www.izotope.com/en/learn/5-tips-for-balancing-edm-kicks-in-the-mix.html), [Armada – kick & bass methods](https://www.armadamusic.com/university/music-production-articles/how-to-mix-your-kick-and-bass-5-must-know-methods), [EDMProd – gain staging](https://www.edmprod.com/gain-staging/), [Peak Studios – EDM mixing](https://www.peak-studios.de/en/edm-mixingtipps/).
